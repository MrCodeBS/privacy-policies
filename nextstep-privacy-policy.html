NextStep Android App

Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 13, 2026

NextStep stores your tasks and settings locally on your device. Nothing is sent to any server. We don't collect, track, or sell any of your data.

Information we collect

NextStep does not require you to create an account. The app stores your tasks, settings, and preferences locally on your device. This information is never transmitted to our servers or any third party.

Data storage

All task data is stored locally on your device using local storage. We do not collect, upload, or sell your personal task information. You remain in full control of your data at all times.

Analytics and tracking

NextStep does not use analytics or tracking technologies. We do not monitor how you use the app.

Advertising

NextStep does not display third-party advertisements.

Third-party services

NextStep does not currently integrate any third-party services. If future versions include optional features such as cloud backup or push notifications, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those features are made available.

Data security

Because your information is stored locally on your device, you remain in control of it. We recommend protecting your device with a screen lock or other appropriate security measures.

Children's privacy

NextStep is not directed at children under the age required by applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the effective date at the top of this page.
